# Review samples

Temporary files for reviewing the polish pass. Delete this folder before merging.

- `voice/`: the same three lines recorded with three ElevenLabs models
  (`eleven_multilingual_v2` is what the apps currently use; `eleven_v3` and `eleven_v4` are the alternatives).
- `screens/`: every screen of both apps at the 11-inch iPad sizes, with the new art.
  Shark screenshots are from a standard iPad simulator, Cat Math from an 11-inch iPad Pro simulator.

The art itself is in `apps/cat-math/Resources/Art` and `apps/shark-explorer/Resources/Art/ocean-map.png`.
