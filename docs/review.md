picture or settle a fact. This file lists what shipped without that check. Tick an item off by deleting it;
add a section whenever content ships unheard or unverified.

## Facts

Every fact was taken from a fetched page listed in the animal's `sourceURLs`, but the quotes behind the Ancient
Seas facts were captured through a page-summarising tool and not compared word for word.

- **Cladoselache** is called an early shark; some scientists now place it nearer the ratfish. The game says so
  once, on its Fossil Clues page.
- **Spiny dogfish** lifespan uses NOAA's 35 to 40 years; the Florida Museum says 25 to 30 and Oceana over 60.
- **Puffadder shyshark** is called endangered (Two Oceans Aquarium, Save Our Seas); the Florida Museum page
  still says near threatened.
- **Pocket shark:** that it squirts its glowing liquid is NOAA's idea, written as "scientists think". Its food
  is unknown and the page says so.
- **Kitefin:** the oily liver helping it float is confirmed on one page only (Shark Research Institute).
- **Size comparisons are the writers' own**, not from the sources: megalodon and Basilosaurus against buses,
  "as big as a kid" (horn shark), "as long as a ruler" (dogfish pup), and similar.
